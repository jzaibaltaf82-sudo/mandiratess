The atta price in Pakistan today is the retail flour price — what a shop charges for a bag — not the wholesale wheat (gandum) price. The two move together, but atta lags wheat by a few weeks: milling, packing, transport and the shop's margin sit between them.

Why atta costs more than wheat

Wheat in Punjab mandis averaged about Rs 4,546 per 40kg on the Punjab page (AMIS Punjab, 2 Nov 2026) — about Rs 113.65 per kg. Atta at Rs 147.07 per kg is about Rs 33.42 per kg more. That gap is milling, packing, transport and margin, not a single markup. Raw wheat for a 20kg bag at the mandi rate would cost about Rs 2,273; the PBS retail average for the finished 20kg bag is Rs 2,941.34. When the mandi rate moves, expect atta to follow a few weeks later — do not copy a mandi move into atta the same day.

Chakki atta — what it costs

Chakki atta usually costs more than mill atta: smaller batches plus a grinding charge. Take your own wheat to a chakki and you pay wheat plus grinding, not retail atta. Formula: (wheat price per kg × kg) + grinding charge = chakki atta cost. At the Punjab mandi average (Rs 113.65/kg), 20kg of wheat costs about Rs 2,273 before grinding. No verified city retail chakki per-kg figure is published here — do not invent one.
