Fresh milk was unchanged on the week and is 8.93% dearer than a year ago; NIDO fell 0.36% on the week and is 1.18% dearer than a year ago.

The milk price in Pakistan today is two prices: loose fresh milk by the litre, and powdered milk by the pack. Fresh milk is sold loose by the litre in most shops and is the price that moves with fodder and the seasons; powdered milk is a branded pack whose price moves slowly. This page gives the dated PBS figures for both, with the city range.

How to read these prices

Loose milk is the fresh-milk price. The PBS surveys loose, un-boiled milk as sold by the litre. Packaged or branded milk (UHT or pasteurised, in cartons) costs more than the loose average — do not compare a carton price with Rs 221.05 and call the difference a price rise.

Fresh milk is flat this week — that is normal. Loose milk moves slowly because it is a daily staple with local supply. A week with no change (0.00%) is common; the 8.93% yearly rise is the real trend, driven by fodder costs.

NIDO is a pack price, not a per-kg price. The PBS price is for the 390g polybag. Per kg it is about Rs 2,901.77 — but nobody buys powdered milk by the kg loose, so the pack price is the number to use.

City differences are big for fresh milk. The range is Rs 170–280 per litre — a litre in Peshawar (Rs 266.46) costs about 43% more than in Bahawalpur (Rs 186.43). Fresh milk is local, so transport and local fodder decide the city price.

What could move milk prices next

  • The next PBS SPI, for the week ended 19 Nov, expected Fri 20 Nov 2026. Fresh milk was flat this week; the next release shows whether it stays flat or the winter fodder season starts to bite.
  • Fodder costs. Green fodder and feed prices move the loose-milk price with a lag — the same grain market that moves wheat and chicken feed.
  • Winter supply. Milk supply usually rises in winter, which can hold the price steady even as fodder costs rise; a poor fodder season does the opposite.
  • Pack prices. NIDO is a branded pack — its price moves when the company revises its rate, not with the weekly milk market. A 0.36% weekly fall is a survey average, not a company price cut.

Frequently asked questions

What is the milk price in Pakistan today?

Fresh milk Rs 221.05 per litre on the PBS national average (week ended 12 Nov 2026), unchanged on the week and 8.93% dearer than a year ago. Packaged or branded milk costs more than the loose average.

What is the price of NIDO milk in Pakistan?

Rs 1,131.69 for the 390g pack on the PBS national average (week ended 12 Nov 2026), down 0.36% on the week and up 1.18% on the year. That is about Rs 290.18 per 100g.

What is the milk price per litre in Lahore / Karachi / Peshawar?

Lahore Rs 218.02, Karachi Rs 240.00 and Peshawar Rs 266.46 per litre (PBS Annex, week ended 12 Nov 2026). Fresh milk varies widely by city — Peshawar is dearest of these three.

Why is milk cheaper in Bahawalpur than Peshawar?

Bahawalpur averaged Rs 186.43 per litre against Peshawar's Rs 266.46 in the same week (PBS Annex). Fresh milk is local, so local fodder, supply and transport decide the city price; the PBS release does not give a cause for the gap.
