What the history does — and does not — say yet

  • Mandi: one full AMIS Punjab release (2 Nov 2026) plus two partial snapshots (5 May 2026, 18 mandis; 20 Nov 2026, 12 mandis) are in hand. A 7-day and 30-day chart needs daily releases; until they accumulate, the anchor dots above and the range bar on the hub are the honest picture.
  • Atta: nineteen weekly PBS points are in hand (19 Feb–19 Nov 2026), with gaps where no bulletin was found. The next point arrives with the SPI for the week ended 26 Nov, expected Fri 27 Nov 2026.
  • Policy: the indicative Rs 4,500 for the 2027 crop (23 Oct 2026) follows Rs 3,500 for 2025-26 — a rise of Rs 1,000, about 29%. The Rs 3,900 figure for an earlier season is not published here: its season label is unverified.
  • Season: prices are usually lowest just after harvest (April–May) and higher later as stocks fall. That is a seasonal pattern, not a forecast for any specific week.

About MandiRates

MandiRates (mandiratess.com) publishes dated, sourced mandi and retail prices for Pakistan's main crops — wheat first, then rice, sugar and cotton, plus the daily essentials (daal, sabzi, chicken and eggs, milk, ghee and cooking oil, LPG, petrol and diesel) from the PBS weekly bulletin — for farmers, traders and households.
