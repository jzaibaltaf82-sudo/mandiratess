Frequently asked questions

Where do MandiRates prices come from?

Mandi rates come from AMIS Punjab; retail and city prices come from the PBS Sensitive Price Indicator (weekly, Fridays). Every figure on this site names its source and date — nothing is copied from other price sites.

Why is there no single wheat price for Pakistan?

Rates change by mandi, quality and city — the 2 Nov 2026 AMIS release ranged from Rs 3,363 in Rahim Yar Khan to Rs 5,640 in Rawalpindi. This site shows the range and the city breakdown, never one settled “today” number.

How often are prices updated?

PBS prices update weekly with the Friday SPI bulletin (next: week ended 26 Nov, expected Fri 27 Nov 2026). AMIS mandi prices update on weekdays when a new release appears; the latest full release is 2 Nov 2026, with a 12-mandi snapshot from 20 Nov 2026.

About the two Punjab averages. Zarat Ghar's hub page states Rs 4,551 per 40kg and its Punjab page states Rs 4,546 per 40kg for the same AMIS release of 2 Nov 2026. Recomputing the 19 Punjab mandis on the Punjab page (total Rs 86,373 ÷ 19) gives Rs 4,545.95, i.e. Rs 4,546. The Punjab page on this site uses Rs 4,546; the wheat hub page keeps Rs 4,551 with this footnote. The difference is Rs 5 per 40kg — about 13 paisa per kg.
