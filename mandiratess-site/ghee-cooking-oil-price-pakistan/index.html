The ghee and cooking oil price in Pakistan today is four prices, not one: vegetable ghee in a 2.5kg tin, vegetable ghee in a 1kg pouch, cooking oil in a 5-litre tin, and mustard oil by the kg. The PBS tracks all four every week, and this page gives the dated figures with the city range — so the tin, pouch or litre you are buying is the one you are pricing.

How to read these prices

Pack size decides the per-kg price. The 2.5kg ghee tin costs Rs 633.82 per kg, while the 1kg pouch costs Rs 621.73 per kg — the same ghee at a different per-kg rate because the packs differ. Always divide by the pack weight before comparing two ghee prices.

Cooking oil is per litre, not per kg. The 5-litre tin is Rs 3,101.17, about Rs 620.23 per litre. A litre of oil weighs less than a kg, so do not compare a per-litre oil price with a per-kg ghee price as if they were the same unit.

Mustard oil is loose. Unlike the branded ghee and cooking oil packs, mustard oil is sold loose by the kg and its price varies by city and by quality — Rs 480–720 per kg nationally. A shop's mustard oil at Rs 650 per kg is inside the published range.

All four rose 5–7% on the year. Ghee 2.5kg is up 6.15% on the year (from Rs 1,492.75), ghee 1kg up 6.77% (from Rs 582.32), cooking oil up 5.78% (from Rs 2,931.76) and mustard oil up 5.79% (from Rs 540.21). The weekly moves — all under 0.5% — are small; the yearly rise is the real trend.

What could move ghee and cooking oil prices next

  • The next PBS SPI, for the week ended 19 Nov, expected Fri 20 Nov 2026. All four items moved less than 0.5% this week; the next release shows whether the slow yearly climb continues.
  • Palm oil imports. Pakistan imports most of its palm oil, so the world palm price and the rupee move the ghee and cooking oil rate more than local factors do.
  • Mustard crop. Mustard is a Rabi crop — its local harvest decides how much of the mustard oil market is local and how much is imported.
  • Ramadan demand. Ghee and cooking oil demand rises before Ramadan, and prices usually follow — the seasonal event to watch.

What is the cooking oil price in Pakistan today?
