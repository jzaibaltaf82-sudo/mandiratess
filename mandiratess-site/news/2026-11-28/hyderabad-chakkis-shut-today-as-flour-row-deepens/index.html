Hyderabad chakkis shut today as flour row deepens

Checked by Jahanzaib, MandiRates · Published 28 November 2026 · Every figure in this post names its source and date.

Daily commodity news — 28 November 2026. Prices in this story come only from that day's AMIS or PBS release.

Hyderabad's traditional flour mills (chakkis) shut completely today, Saturday, after talks with the district administration failed. The Sindh government has fixed roller-mill flour at Rs 145 a kg retail (ex-mill Rs 138) until 31 January 2027, but shops were selling it at Rs 175 on Thursday — Rs 30 above the notified rate — while chakki flour sold at Rs 190–200 a kg. Chakki owners say their government wheat quota of 60kg per grinding stone a day is far below the 2,000kg a chakki can grind, and they want their production-cost assessment revised after the government raised roller mills' processing cost from Rs 16 to Rs 26 a kg. A 100kg wheat sack eased Rs 200 to Rs 15,400 in Hyderabad's open market on Thursday.

Source: Express Tribune, 28 Nov 2026
