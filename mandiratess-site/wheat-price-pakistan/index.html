Official price vs market price — why the numbers differ

Three prices are often quoted as “the wheat price”, and they are not the same. The mandi price is the wholesale price in the grain market — about Rs 4,551 per 40kg on average, range Rs 3,363–5,640, dated 2 Nov 2026 (AMIS Punjab). The government indicative / support price is the price announced for the next crop — Rs 4,500 per 40kg for the 2027 crop, set on 23 Oct 2026. It is a policy signal for sowing and procurement, not the price you pay in a mandi today. The retail price is what a shop charges. It is higher than the mandi price because it includes transport, storage, packing, and margin.

When two sites differ on the same day, check the date, the unit, and whether the figure is mandi, retail, or government. A site showing Rs 4,400–4,520 in one place and Rs 3,850–4,450 in another has a data problem, not a price problem. This page does not copy competitor numbers — every figure names its source and date, and conflicts are shown as a range.

How the wheat price is calculated — units explained

Most confusion about the gandum rate comes from units. Per 40kg is the standard mandi unit. Punjab mandi prices are quoted per 40kg — Rs 4,551 per 40kg (AMIS Punjab, 2 Nov 2026). Always check the unit before comparing two prices. Per kg is the retail unit. Divide a 40kg price by 40 to get per kg: Rs 4,551 ÷ 40 = about Rs 113.78 per kg. Multiply a per-kg price by 40 to get the 40kg price. 40kg is not the same as a maund everywhere. In Punjab, a maund is commonly treated as 40kg for wheat. In other contexts — cotton is one example — a maund is 37.324kg. If a price is quoted “per maund” without stating the weight, ask which maund it is. Per 100kg is a bulk unit. Multiply the per-kg price by 100, or the per-40kg price by 2.5 — at Rs 113.78 per kg, 100kg is about Rs 11,378. Bag sizes are not prices. Compare bags per kg: the PBS average of Rs 2,941.34 for a 20kg bag (PBS SPI, week ended 5 Nov 2026) is about Rs 147.07 per kg; a 50kg bag at the same rate would be about Rs 7,353.35.
