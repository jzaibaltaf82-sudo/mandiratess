LPG gas cylinder price in Pakistan today

Checked by Jahanzaib, MandiRates · Updated 20 Nov 2026, 17:45 PKT · Every figure on this page names its source and date.

LPG — PBS NATIONAL AVERAGE (WEEK ENDED 19 NOV 2026)

11.67kg cylinder Rs 6,095.87 (about Rs 522.35 / kg)

Down 6.46% on the week and 87.99% dearer than a year ago — the biggest yearly rise of any item in the PBS bulletin.

The LPG gas cylinder price in Pakistan today is the price most households actually pay for a domestic cylinder — and it is also the price with the biggest gap between the official figures. The PBS surveys what shops charged in 17 cities and found an average of Rs 6,095.87 for an 11.67kg cylinder. OGRA, the regulator, notified a maximum consumer price of Rs 294.07 per kg for November 2026 — an 11.8kg cylinder at that rate is Rs 3,470.09. Those two numbers do not agree, and this page does not pretend they do. The PBS figure is the page's source for the market price; the OGRA figure is shown honestly alongside it, with the difference explained below.

Why the two figures differ. OGRA's Rs 294.07 per kg is the notified maximum consumer price — a ceiling set by the regulator for November 2026, up from Rs 279.34 per kg in October (11.8kg cylinder Rs 3,296.23). The PBS figure of Rs 6,095.87 is the surveyed average of what shops actually charged in 17 cities in the week ended 19 Nov 2026 — about Rs 522.35 per kg, well above the OGRA ceiling. The PBS and OGRA releases themselves do not explain the gap, and this page does not invent an explanation for it. What is certain is that the market price households pay is far above the notified maximum, and a reader who quotes only one of the two figures is quoting half the story.

The per-kg PBS figure (Rs 522.35) is calculated from the cylinder price: Rs 6,095.87 ÷ 11.67kg. The OGRA 11.8kg cylinder figure (Rs 3,470.09) is OGRA's own notified figure, not a calculation from the per-kg rate.

How to read these prices

The cylinder size matters. The PBS surveys an 11.67kg cylinder; OGRA's standard cylinder is 11.8kg. A 0.13kg difference is small, but it is why the per-kg rate — not the cylinder total — is the fair comparison between the two figures.

The yearly rise is the real story. A year ago the PBS average was Rs 3,242.61 for the same cylinder. It is now Rs 6,095.87 — up 87.99% in a year, nearly double. The weekly move was -6.46% (from Rs 6,517.08), so this week's price fell back from last week's level; it is still far above the level of a year ago.

Winter is the dear season. LPG demand rises in winter, when households use it for cooking and heating and piped gas supply is short. The November price is already high before the coldest weeks arrive.

Check the cylinder before paying. A cylinder sold below the PBS average is not necessarily a bargain — it may be under-filled. The PBS figure is the surveyed average for a full 11.67kg cylinder.

What could move LPG prices next

  • OGRA's December notification, on 1 Dec 2026. OGRA revises the notified maximum every month. The November figure was Rs 294.07 per kg; December's figure is the next official data point.
  • The next PBS SPI, for the week ended 26 Nov, expected Fri 27 Nov 2026. The last release showed a 6.46% weekly fall — the next release shows whether the market price is holding, rising, or falling back toward the OGRA ceiling.
  • Supply. LPG is largely imported, so the rupee, world LPG prices and border conditions feed into the shop price within weeks. Any supply disruption shows up first in the city prices, then in the national average.
  • Winter demand. The coldest weeks are still ahead. Demand rises fastest in December and January, and that is when the gap between the notified maximum and the shop price usually matters most.
