PBS average vs OGRA notified rate. The PBS weekly average and OGRA's notified rate can differ, because they are measured on different dates: OGRA now revises the notified price daily — the 24 Nov 2026 rates cover one day (24 Nov) — while the PBS averages the prices surveyed during the whole week. The figures on this page are the PBS weekly averages; if the pump price near you is a few rupees different, the OGRA notified rate in force today (see below) is the figure to compare it with.

How to read these prices

The weekly fall is real, and it is big. Petrol fell 3.87% in one week, the second weekly fall in a row after the 7.42% fall the week before. It follows an OGRA revision; the PBS weekly average captures the week in which the new rate took effect. A fall this size shows up at the pump, not just in the average.

Yearly, both fuels are still much dearer. Petrol is 21.35% dearer than a year ago (Rs 267.15) and diesel 24.84% dearer (Rs 286.20). One good week does not undo a year's rise — the yearly figure is the one that matters for household budgets.

Super is the PBS grade. The PBS tracks "Petrol Super" — the standard grade sold at most pumps. Hi-octane costs more and is not in this table. Do not compare a hi-octane pump price with Rs 324.18 and call the difference a markup.

City differences are small. Unlike sabzi or milk, fuel prices vary by only a few rupees across the country, because the notified rate is national. A pump charging Rs 10 more than the average is worth questioning; a pump charging Rs 2 more is not.

What could move petrol and diesel prices next

  • OGRA's next revision. OGRA has moved to short-cycle notifications — the 21 Nov rates run to 23 Nov, with the next notification due 24 Nov 2026. The PBS weekly average follows the notified rate with a lag of days, not weeks.
  • World oil prices and the rupee. Both fuels are imported, so the world crude price and the rupee decide the next revision. A stronger rupee or cheaper crude shows up at the pump within days.
  • The next PBS SPI, for the week ended 26 Nov, expected Fri 27 Nov 2026. The last two releases captured falls; the next one shows whether the new, lower level holds.
